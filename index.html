BEZEN

BEZEN reads what a person is protecting behind their words — before an agent answers them.

The code, not the model, checks every quote: a reading whose quote is not found in the original is marked unverified and never shown as fact.

One small core. Any number of apps. Each app is a profile and a page — nothing more.

Reply Reader

A customer replied to your message. Find out what they are protecting before you write back.
Kid Message

A child wrote to a parent or a teacher. See what they are guarding and how to open the door without closing it.

Built on the BEZEN skeleton — open spec, open code.
